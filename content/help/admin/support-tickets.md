## Pick up a case

Open AI Support Tickets and choose **Manage ticket**. Read the customer's question, saved context and any authorized conversation evidence. Use **Claim ticket** so the team knows who is handling it. Select the appropriate priority and who the case is waiting on.

## Reply and manage waiting

Write a useful reply and choose **Save reply**. A saved reply is not proof that an email arrived in the customer's inbox. **Mark waiting**, **Snooze** and the UTC snooze field describe work state; use them deliberately rather than closing a case that still needs attention.

If another staff change happened, reload the ticket before saving. The version check protects against overwriting newer work. Use **Release** when another team member should take over.

## Close or reopen honestly

Choose **Close** only when the team's case work is complete; **Reopen** returns it for further attention. Ticket closure and a customer's explicit feedback on an AI answer are separate records. A silent or unrated chat is not confirmed success.

Use Contacts for contact-form submissions. Do not expose another person's ticket or conversation to the requester. Private exports should remain within authorized staff handling.
