Welcome to **Sri Panchami Spiritual**! If you're new to our platform, this guide explains how our wallet credit system works so you can start your astrology journey with confidence.

## What Are Wallet Credits?

Wallet credits are the currency used on our platform for all services — astrology consultations, product purchases, and special services. Think of them as prepaid tokens that make transactions simple and seamless.

## How to Get Credits

1. **Create your account** — [Register here](/register) for free
2. **Go to your Wallet** — Access it from your [account dashboard](/account/dashboard/wallet)
3. **Choose an amount** — Select from available recharge packages
4. **Make payment** — Secure payment via Razorpay (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking, or wallet)
5. **Start using credits** — instantly available in your wallet

## Astrology Session Pricing

### Text Sessions
- **Pricing**: Varies by astrologer (typically 5-10 credits per message)
- **Best for**: Detailed questions, ongoing guidance, non-urgent matters
- **Flexibility**: Send messages at your convenience — the astrologer responds when available

### Direct Call Sessions
- **Pricing**: Varies by astrologer (typically 0.5-1 credit per second)
- **Best for**: Immediate answers, deep conversations, emotional support
- **Cost example**: A 10-minute call at 0.5 credits/second = 300 credits

## Understanding Astrologer Rates

Each astrologer sets their own rates based on:
- **Experience**: Years of practice and expertise
- **Specialization**: Unique areas of mastery
- **Languages**: Available in Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu, and more
- **Availability**: Online status and response time

Compare astrologers on our [consult page](/consult) to find the right match for your needs and budget.

## Tips to Get the Best Value

1. **Prepare your questions** in advance — saves credits by being focused
2. **Start with text sessions** — more economical for initial questions
3. **Use call sessions** when you need deeper, real-time guidance
4. **Monitor your balance** from your wallet dashboard
5. **Recharge during promotions** for bonus credits

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do credits expire?**
A: Credits do not expire and remain in your wallet indefinitely.

**Q: Can I transfer credits to another user?**
A: Currently, credits are non-transferable between accounts.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with a session?**
A: Contact our support team — we review each case individually.

**Q: Is my payment secure?**
A: Yes, all payments are processed through Razorpay with bank-grade encryption.
