Online astrology consultations have made Vedic wisdom accessible to people worldwide. Whether you're seeking guidance on career, relationships, health, or spiritual growth, a well-prepared consultation can provide life-changing insights.

## Before Your Consultation

### Gather Your Birth Details
For an accurate birth chart analysis, you need:

- **Date of birth** (exact date)
- **Time of birth** (as precise as possible — even the exact minute matters)
- **Place of birth** (city and country)

Don't worry if you don't have the exact time — our astrologers can still provide valuable insights using alternative methods like Prashna (horary) astrology.

### Prepare Your Questions
Thoughtful questions lead to meaningful answers. Examples:

- Career: "What career path aligns with my planetary strengths?"
- Relationships: "What should I look for in a life partner?"
- Finances: "What's the best timing for major financial decisions?"
- Health: "Are there specific areas of health I should be mindful of?"
- Spiritual: "What's my dharma or life purpose?"

### Set Your Intention
Take a few minutes to meditate or reflect on what you truly want from the session. Clear intention creates space for clear guidance.

## During the Consultation

- **Be open and honest**: The more you share, the more accurate the guidance
- **Take notes**: Insights often unfold in layers — writing helps you revisit later
- **Ask for clarification**: If something doesn't make sense, ask
- **Stay present**: Put aside distractions and be fully engaged

## After the Consultation

- **Review your notes** within 24 hours while insights are fresh
- **Follow recommended remedies** consistently for best results
- **Journal your progress** — notice how planetary guidance unfolds
- **Schedule follow-ups** when major transits approach
