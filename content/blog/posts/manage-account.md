## Open your account

[Sign in](/login) with the account used for your order, then open [My Orders](/account/dashboard/orders). The account navigation also provides **Saved Addresses**, **My Profile** where available, and app-install guidance. Your account shows your own records, not another customer's.

## Review an order

Choose an order to inspect its items, delivery address, current status and any courier or tracking information. The invoice link opens the recorded invoice when available. A blank list does not prove a payment failed: check the checkout email and any payment reference before contacting support.

Do not pay again merely because a confirmation page or email is delayed. Follow the [payments and order confirmation guide](/blog/payments-and-orders).

## Maintain delivery addresses

Open [Saved Addresses](/account/dashboard/addresses). Use **Add an address** or edit an existing address, give it a useful label, check the recipient, phone, street, city, state and PIN code, then select **Save address**. Choose **Use by default at checkout** when appropriate.

**Remove** deletes the selected saved-address entry after confirmation. A change to your saved addresses does not rewrite an order already placed; contact the team if that order needs attention.

## Update your profile or password

Your profile shows your saved account photo when it loads. If the photo is unavailable, you’ll see an initial beside your email.

Open [My Profile](/account/dashboard/profile), review the supported personal fields and choose **Save profile**. Your login email is not changed by that form.

Where **Change password** is offered, enter your current password and matching new passwords, then save. Accounts signed in through another provider may not have a local password-change form. Use the appropriate sign-in or [recovery flow](/forgot-password), and never share a password with support.

## Get help safely

Use the [support guide](/blog/get-support) when the correct order or account still cannot be found. Include the page and relevant reference, but never a password, bank PIN, one-time login code or full payment-card details.
