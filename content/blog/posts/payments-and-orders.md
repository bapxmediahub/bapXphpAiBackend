## Product payments

Product orders use the secure live payment option shown at checkout. Use **Order review** to check the amount before approving payment. On a phone, **Review order** jumps to the summary. Check the delivery address in **Shipping details**.

## After payment

The website verifies the payment before creating the order. Do not repeat payment if you receive a reference but the page is delayed. Check **My Orders** first, then contact support with the payment reference.

## Check before retrying

Review the exact amount, delivery address and payment reference. If a gateway reports success but the site does not yet show an order, keep that reference and the safe error message. Check the account used at checkout and [contact the team](/contact) when the outcome is uncertain. Do not create another payment merely to test whether the first one worked.

## Email and order records are different evidence

A confirmation email can be delayed or filtered. Check the correct inbox and spam folder, but do not assume that an email alone replaces the verified order record. An email accepted by a sending server is not proof that it reached your inbox.

Open the confirmed order to inspect its items, address, delivery state and available invoice. Courier tracking becomes useful only when the record supplies it. Requests to correct a placed order or investigate a refund belong with the team; changing a saved address does not alter an earlier order.
