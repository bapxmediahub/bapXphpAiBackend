## Ask the Support Agent

Open **Support** on a page where the chat button is available. Ask about a specific product, collection, delivery policy or order question. Give the actual item name or link when possible. If a product is not verified or a collection is unclear, the agent may ask you to clarify instead of substituting unrelated items.

The agent can make mistakes. Check the product page, current checkout information and the relevant policy before acting on an answer. A chat response does not itself place an order, issue a refund or change your account.

## Check private order or ticket information

[Sign in](/login) before asking about your own support-ticket status or opening [My Orders](/account/dashboard/orders). Ask a clear question such as “show my latest support ticket status”. The site uses your signed-in identity; entering another person's email or reference does not grant access to their information.

If the application confirms that it created a support ticket, keep the displayed reference. Do not repeatedly submit the same problem while a request is still running or its outcome is uncertain.

## Tell us whether the answer helped

When the reply offers **Yes, resolved** or **No, need help**, choose the option that matches your experience. This records feedback on that answer. It is not the same as an email receipt, payment confirmation or a human team's ticket status.

A new question can need further help even after an earlier answer was useful. An unanswered feedback prompt does not mean your case is solved.

## Contact the human team

Use the [contact page](/contact) for help from the team. Include the relevant product link, order or payment reference, the page you used, what you expected and the safe error message you saw. For account tasks, consult the [account guide](/blog/manage-account).

Never send your password, bank PIN, one-time authentication code, full card details or another customer's private information. If payment confirmation is delayed, [check the order guidance](/blog/payments-and-orders) before trying to pay again. Response and delivery times must be confirmed by the team; a submitted message is not proof that someone has already read it.
