# Create and access your account

## Create an account

1. Select **Login** in the top navigation.
2. Choose **Create account**.
3. Enter your name, email address, and a secure password.
4. Accept the Terms of Service and select **Create account**.

Your account receives **25 welcome credits**. These credits can be used for consultant messages and calls.

## Sign in

Open **Login**, enter your email and password, and select **Sign in**. After signing in, **Dashboard** appears in the navigation.

Your dashboard contains your orders, consultation sessions, wallet balance, and transaction history.

## Reset your password

Select **Forgot password?** on the login page. Enter your account email and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. Reset links expire for your protection.
