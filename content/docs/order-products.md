# Order products and manage your cart

## Add a product

1. Open **Shop** and choose a product.
2. Use the **plus** control to add one item. The quantity begins at zero.
3. Continue selecting plus or minus to set the quantity you need.
4. Open the cart icon in the top navigation to review the order.

Reducing an item to zero removes it from the cart. Your subtotal updates without reloading the full page.

## Checkout

Select **Proceed to Checkout** from the cart. Enter your name, email, phone number, delivery address, city, and PIN code.

When you are signed in, you can choose a previously saved delivery address. Give each new address a recognizable name such as **Home** or **Office** so it is easy to reuse.

## After payment

Successful orders appear under **Dashboard → My Orders**. Keep the payment confirmation until the order appears in your account.
