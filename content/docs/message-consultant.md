# Message a consultant

## Start a request

1. Sign in and open **Consult**.
2. Choose the message icon on the consultant card.
3. If the consultant is available, the session request starts immediately.
4. If the consultant is offline or busy, your request joins their queue without charging credits.

You are charged the displayed message rate only when the consultant accepts your queued request.

## Use the private room

Open **Dashboard → My Sessions** and select the session. Messaging becomes available after the consultant accepts. Messages are visible only to the customer and the assigned consultant.

Keep the session page open while waiting. Its status updates automatically from queued to accepted or active.
