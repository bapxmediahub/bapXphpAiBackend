# Wallet credits and payments

## Check your balance

Open **Dashboard → Wallet** to see your current credits and transaction history. Product orders and consultation credits are separate: wallet credits are only for consultant message and call sessions.

## Recharge

Enter the amount you want to add and review the service charge, tax, total payable, and credits you will receive. Select the payment button only when the page shows that live payments are available.

After successful gateway verification, the transaction changes from pending to confirmed and the credits appear once. Refreshing the page does not add the same payment twice.

If payment was taken but credits do not appear, do not pay again. Contact support with the payment reference.
