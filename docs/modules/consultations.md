# Historical Appointment Records

Public consultation booking is retired. `/consult` and `/consult/{slug}` redirect to `/shop`; legacy booking submissions redirect to the general enquiry form.

The product does not expose a public appointment, message, call, wallet, credit, or WebRTC flow.

Historical records remain available to the central administrator at `/admin/appointments`. Only an administrator can update their status.
